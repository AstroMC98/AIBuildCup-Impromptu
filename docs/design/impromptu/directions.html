Recommendation

Go with Live Wire

It is the only direction where the product's two real signals, which episode you're in and who is talking right now, drive the whole screen. The gel follows the vibe and the chapter, the type follows the voice, and the studio clock gives every wait the same honest shape.

  • Borrow Switchboard's lit phone lines for the call-in queue: waiting, next, and on air read at a glance.
  • Borrow Recital's whole-line surtitle fade as the captions mode for reduced motion.
  • Keep Recital's cart and programme ideas out: they're lovely, and they fight the vibe colors.

How to judge these

Watch one phone through a full loop, then switch on Reduced motion and watch again. A direction that still reads well when nothing moves is one we can build on.
